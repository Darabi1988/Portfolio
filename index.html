About

The engineer
behind the work.

Simulation & Development Engineer with 12+ years across industrial R&D, academic research, and product development. Based in Bremen, Germany.

I'm a simulation and development engineer with over 12 years of experience in structural mechanics, fatigue analysis, and data-driven engineering. My career spans fracture mechanics laboratories in Tehran, research at Universität Stuttgart, and industrial engineering at Springer GmbH in Germany.

What drives me is the intersection of rigorous simulation and real engineering impact — whether that's cutting component weight by 25%, extending fatigue life by 40%, or building machine learning pipelines that significantly reduce physical testing requirements.

I approach every project from design concept through to validated results. My work combines deep technical expertise in FEM (Abaqus, ANSYS, Nastran) with modern data science tools (Python, ANN, deep learning) to deliver engineering solutions that are both rigorous and efficient.

Currently open to senior engineering roles in structural simulation, R&D, or product development — as well as selected freelance simulation and modelling projects.
