About

The engineer
behind the work

I'm a simulation and development engineer with over 12 years of experience in structural mechanics, fatigue analysis, and data-driven engineering. My career has taken me from fracture mechanics laboratories in Tehran to research at Universität Stuttgart and industrial development at Springer GmbH in Germany.

What drives me is the intersection of rigorous simulation and real engineering impact — whether that's cutting component weight by 25%, extending fatigue life by 40%, or building machine learning pipelines that reduce the need for physical tests. I approach every project from design concept through to validated results.

I'm currently open to senior engineering roles in structural simulation, R&D, or product development, as well as freelance FEM and data-driven modelling projects.
