About

The engineer
behind the work

I'm a mechanical engineer with a strong focus on computational simulation and precision CAD design. My work spans from structural integrity assessments of industrial equipment to fluid dynamics studies for product optimisation.

I believe that great engineering is part rigour, part creativity — and that the most valuable simulations are those that directly answer a real design question. Whether working within a team or independently on a freelance brief, I aim to deliver results that are clear, reproducible, and well-documented.

Currently open to full-time roles in R&D or analysis engineering, as well as freelance simulation and modelling projects.
