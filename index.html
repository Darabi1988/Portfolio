Lightweight Redesign of Industrial Robotic Gripper Systems

Problem

Large-scale gripper components were over-engineered — excess mass increased operational loads and manufacturing costs with no structural benefit.

My Role & Process

Led full redesign cycle: CAD geometry optimisation → FEM stress & fatigue analysis (Abaqus, ANSYS) per FKM guidelines → iterative mass reduction → experimental structural validation. Managed technical coordination across engineering, production, and suppliers.

Results

Data-Driven Material Modelling with Deep Learning & FEM

Problem

Characterising mechanical properties through physical experiments was slow, expensive, and limited to discrete parameter combinations.

My Role & Process

Built fully automated simulation pipeline: phase-field modelling (PFM) + FEM + Python post-processing. Trained ANN & deep learning models for material property prediction and damage initiation. Applied inverse design methods to map process parameters to target properties — cutting experimental effort significantly.
